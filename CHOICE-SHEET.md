# The choice sheet

From 15:00 to 15:30 you fill it in on the platform, on the Choices page: a single text, up to
20,000 characters, that you can save and correct until the freeze. The jury reads it if you make
the top 6, next to your CRM: it is one of the three things the jury scores.

This is the template: copy it, remove the explanation lines in italics and write under each
question. What counts is what can be checked: the file and the column, an example row, how many
rows it touches, what your CRM does. You can start from your agent's notes, but the sheet is
yours: the jury can ask you to explain it.

---

## 1. What you found in the data that the requests didn't say

*One block per case, the most important first. If a case spans several files, say so.*

**Case: _(a short name)_**

- **Where**: _(files and columns)_
- **How it's written**: _(one or two example rows, copied from the export)_
- **How many rows**: _(roughly, in the export you got)_
- **How you noticed**: _(what you looked at, what didn't add up)_

## 2. How you handled it and why

*For each case of section 1, with the same name.*

**Case: _(the same name)_**

- **What your CRM does**: _(the rule you apply, in one sentence)_
- **Why this reading**: _(what in the data or in the requests justifies it)_
- **What you ruled out**: _(the other possible readings, and why not)_
- **How you verified it**: _(a check, a count, a test)_

## 3. What you didn't do

*What is missing or half done, and why: choosing priorities is part of the race.*

- **Requests left out or half done**: _(which ones, and what is missing)_
- **Cases seen and not handled**: _(which ones, and what happens to those rows today)_
- **What you would do with one more hour**: _(in order)_

## 4. The assistant

- **What it can do**: _(the requests it handles)_
- **How it works**: _(tools, how it reads the CRM, how it writes)_
- **When it asks, when it says no**: _(how it decides that a request must not be carried out as
  it stands)_
- **How you avoid damage**: _(how it makes sure it touches only the right records)_
