# Normalized Sinergia Export

Generated by `python normalize_export.py` from `legacy/export/sinergia-export`.

The source CSVs are not modified. These files are additive normalized exports intended to feed the CRM migration layer while preserving legacy identifiers and raw fields where meaning could otherwise be lost.

## Files

- `companies.normalized.csv` - company fields, normalized domain, extracted `partita_iva`, deleted flag, parsed modification date.
- `contacts.normalized.csv` - contact names, validated lowercase email, raw email, lifecycle stage, deleted flag.
- `deals.normalized.csv` - deal amount, currency, pipeline, stage, close date, split contact IDs, active-user owner only, raw amount/currency/stage fields.
- `products.normalized.csv` - normalized SKU, price, raw SKU/price, deleted flag.
- `line_items.normalized.csv` - normalized SKU, raw SKU/price, quantity, unit price, discount percentage.
- `tickets.normalized.csv` - ticket text, canonical pipeline/stage/priority, raw status/priority, dates, active-user assignee only, deleted flag.
- `activities.normalized.csv` - activity object type, timestamp, body, contact/deal/user references, deleted flag.
- `users.normalized.csv` - user identity, normalized email, active flag.
- `stage_history.normalized.csv` - raw old/new stage labels and parsed change date.

## Conservative Choices

- Rows marked deleted are retained with `is_deleted=true`; the migration layer can skip them without losing auditability.
- Invalid or ambiguous normalized values are left blank rather than guessed.
- Raw fields are retained for deals where parsing changes meaning-sensitive values: `raw_importo`, `raw_valuta`, and `raw_fase`.
- Dedupe/merge is not performed in these files. The normalized keys needed for dedupe are present, but merge rules should run in the migration layer so associations can be remapped consistently.
- R8 revenue and R9 dormant-customer calculations are not materialized here; they require associations after migration.

## Normalization Rules

- Deleted markers `S`, `SI`, accented Italian yes, and `1` are recognized case-insensitively.
- Amounts use decimal arithmetic, validated thousands grouping, and cent rounding. Unknown text is rejected. `k`/`mila` mean thousands; `mln`/`milione`/`milioni` mean millions. Monthly renewal values (`mensile`, `mensili`, `al mese`, `/mese`) are multiplied by 12.
- Dates keep their supplied date/time precision without inventing a timezone. Two-digit years below 50 mean 2000-2049; other two-digit years mean 1950-1999. Excel serial dates must exceed 10000; invalid or oversized values are blank.
- Observed SKU aliases (`BF12345`, `BF.12345`, `BF 12345`, `art. 12345`, and numeric-only codes) become `BF-12345`, with five-digit padding. Unrecognized codes retain their original text.
- Ticket `Nuovo` maps to `aperto`, `Lavorazione` to `in_lavorazione`, waiting aliases to `in_attesa_del_cliente`, and `Risolto` to `chiuso`. Unknown stages stay blank. `status` retains its column name but now contains the canonical stage ID, also provided as `hs_pipeline_stage`; `hs_pipeline` is `assistenza`.
- Ticket priorities map to `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, or `URGENT`; `Normale` means `MEDIUM`. Observed numbered prefixes and trailing exclamation marks are accepted. Missing/unknown priorities are blank. Original labels are retained in `raw_stato` and `raw_priorita`.
