# Two example requests

Two requests as a Brambilla sales rep opens them, in Italian with an English gloss. Who writes and
when go in `context`, as the brief describes.

## Example 1

- **Who writes** (`context.user`): `elena.silvestri@brambillaforniture.it`
- **When** (`context.now`): `2026-12-02T10:00:00+01:00`

> Segna come vinta la trattativa di Nuova Serramenti Mazza, è arrivato l'ordine firmato.

*Mark the Nuova Serramenti Mazza deal as won, the signed order has arrived.*

## Example 2

- **Who writes** (`context.user`): `mattia.vigano@brambillaforniture.it`
- **When** (`context.now`): `2026-12-02T10:00:00+01:00`

> Quanto abbiamo fatturato con Nuova Tessile Spinelli nel 2025?
